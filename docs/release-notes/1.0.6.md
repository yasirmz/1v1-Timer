The timers on your stream.

- OBS's Game Capture records only the game, so the timer window never showed on stream (and a Window Capture of it came out black). 1v1 Timer now gives OBS its own copy: in **Settings → Stream (OBS)**, press **Copy link** and add it in OBS as a **Browser** source (width 700, height 140).
- On stream it shows the same two timers: your colors, Last 20 Seconds, and see-through with Transparent Background. It follows every start, stop and switch at once, wherever you place it.
- The link (`http://127.0.0.1:47123/`) works on your PC only, and costs nothing while OBS is closed.

Update from inside the app (the tray menu or Settings → Check now), or run this setup over your install: it keeps your settings.
