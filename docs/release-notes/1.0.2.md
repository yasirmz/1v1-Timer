PlayStation and other controllers.

- PlayStation controllers (DualSense, DualShock 4) and most other USB and Bluetooth controllers now work as hotkeys, with no Steam or DS4Windows needed. Before, only Xbox controllers did.
- Their buttons count as the Xbox button in the same place: Cross is A, Circle B, Square X, Triangle Y, and the d-pad is the d-pad. Your hotkeys work on every controller as they are.
- A controller that DS4Windows also shows as an Xbox one counts each press once.

Update from inside the app (the tray menu or Settings → Check now), or run this setup over your install: it keeps your settings.
